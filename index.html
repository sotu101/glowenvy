Our Story

Hello, I'm Ivy

My journey with skincare started from a personal struggle.

Years ago, while I was still in school, I used a certain lotion that left me with stretch marks and affected my confidence. I became very conscious of my skin and started searching for products that could help me care for my skin safely without causing more damage.

That experience opened my eyes to how important it is to understand what we put on our skin. Instead of simply looking for products that promised quick results, I became passionate about learning about skincare, ingredients, formulation and what actually works for different skin types.

That passion eventually became Glow Envy by Ivy.

Today, I carefully create and curate skincare solutions with the goal of helping women achieve healthy-looking, radiant and beautiful skin without compromising their skin barrier.

Glow Envy was born from my own skin journey—and today, it exists to help you feel confident in yours. ✨

No unrealistic promises. No shortcuts. Just intentional skincare, carefully selected ingredients and routines created with your skin in mind.

No bleaching. No false promises. Just barrier-first skincare that makes you want to skip foundation.

Cordelia Ivwurie

Founder & Formulator

      Shipping & Returns

      Please read this before placing your order.

      Shipping

      Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Delivery within Lagos usually takes 1-3 business days; other states across Nigeria, 3-7 business days. You'll get a tracking link by email once your order ships.

      Returns policy

      We accept returns within 7 days of delivery, but only for products that are unopened, unused, and in their original packaging with seals intact. Because these are cosmetic and skincare products, we're unable to accept a return or issue a refund on anything once it has been opened — this is for hygiene and safety reasons and applies to every item in the catalog.

      Damaged or incorrect items

      If an order arrives damaged, faulty, or wrong, email us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo and your order number — we'll arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you, even if the item has been opened.

      How to start a return

      Email Helloglowenvyivy@gmail.com with your order number and reason for return. Once approved, pack the unopened item back in its original box and we'll arrange pickup or share a drop-off point.
